STRATEGY DESIGN PATTERN
here inheritence is replaced by composition.
The Strategy Design Pattern is a behavioral design pattern that allows us to define multiple algorithms for performing a particular task, encapsulate each algorithm in a separate class, and make them interchangeable at runtime.

In simple words:

Instead of writing multiple if-else conditions for different behaviours, we create separate classes for each behaviour and choose which one to execute at runtime.
1. Real-world example
Suppose you are developing a payment application like Amazon.

Customers can pay using:
- Credit Card
- UPI
- PayPal
